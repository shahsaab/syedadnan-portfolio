FINANCIAL INFRASTRUCTURE · BANKING 2015 – 2018

RTGS & PRI — Real-Time Interbank Settlement Platform

Bank Alfalah & State Bank of Pakistan · Software Engineer (.NET / Architecture)
⬡ The Context & Architecture

The State Bank of Pakistan issued a strict mandate: commercial banks had to implement a compliant Real-Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) system. Unlike batch processing, RTGS handles large-value interbank settlements in real-time, with immediate finality and zero undo.

Near go-live, the scope expanded to absorb the Pakistan Remittance Initiative (PRI) — turning the platform into a dual-purpose financial backbone routing inbound international remittances away from informal networks.

⚡ The ₨200 Million Pilot Incident

During pilot, an incorrect transaction exceeding ₨200,000,000 (200 Million PKR) was routed. I had built custom real-time WinForms monitoring utilities alongside the core services to track live message flows. Catching the anomaly before PRISM finality allowed me to immediately alert Treasury, who reversed it before settlement.

✓ First to Go Live & Competitor Consultation

Bank Alfalah became the first commercial bank in Pakistan to go live, cleanly passing the stringent SBP regulatory compliance audit. When another top-tier Pakistani bank struggled with their RTGS implementation, their technical leadership reached out to consult with me on solution architecture.

DISTRIBUTED SYSTEMS · HEALTHCARE & RETAIL 2018 – Present

ERA — The 7-Year Distributed Database & AKU Hospital Infrastructure

Aga Khan University Hospital (AKU) & Turf · Co-Founder & Chief Solutions Architect
⬡ Defeating "Lie-Fi" in the Real World

Launching ERA across retail chains revealed a harsh reality: rural network infrastructure suffered chronic drops and degraded connections ("Lie-Fi"). Browser service workers and LocalStorage buckled under complex inventory workloads. In hospital pharmacies operating 24/7, cashier downtime is a critical safety failure.

[ Cloud Master (Azure) ] └── Primary MSSQL Master Database ▲ │ [ Bi-Directional Custom Windows Sync Service ] │ (Stored Procedures, Journal Queue, Conflict Resolution) ▼ [ Edge Terminals: AKU Hospital Pharmacies & Clinics ] ├── Local MSSQL Instance (Zero-Latency Local Transaction Commit) └── WinUI3 / WinForms Terminal (100% Offline Capability)

I stopped fighting browser limits and deployed a local database topology: edge stations run a local MSSQL instance with stored procedure journal queues and background Windows synchronization daemons that reconcile with the cloud master without human intervention.

👥 Mentorship & Delivering "ERA Lite"

When small retail operators needed lightweight cash-desk software, I architected "ERA Lite" in WinForms and mentored Syed Shahriyar Ali, an ambitious 10th-grade apprentice who delivered the core client code under my direction. The trainee shipped a commercial system that generated reliable revenue.

Today, it powers 40+ business locations across Aga Khan University Hospital — South Asia's premier JCI-accredited medical institution — operating continuously 24/7 with zero cash-desk paralysis during telecommunications blackouts.

Engineering Reframe

How 13 Years Without a Safety Net Shapes System Design

When you don't answer to middle management and instead answer directly to central banks, hospital directors, and Fortune 500 executives, your definition of good software changes.

// 01

Production is the Only Test

No certification or classroom credential replaces the experience of routing billions in daily settlement or keeping hospital pharmacies running through regional internet blackouts. Every architectural principle I hold was stress-tested in production.

// 02

Never Tolerate Silent Failures

Silent drops are the fatal flaw of distributed systems. Whether it is Store-and-Forward queues for SWIFT messages or bi-directional SQL sync daemons, failures must be observable, retryable, and surfaced immediately.

// 03

Design for Unstable Realities

Silicon Valley dogma assumes 5G connectivity and unlimited bandwidth. Real-world engineering requires assuming connections will degrade into "Lie-Fi". Resilient edge caching and local autonomous commits protect users when external infrastructure collapses.

// 04

No Black Boxes in Compliance

When central bank auditors and healthcare compliance officers review your architecture, opaque dependencies and bloated libraries become liabilities. Understanding every layer of code you ship guarantees transparency and audit defense.
