CLOUD SERVERLESS & AI PIPELINES · US MARKET 2023 – 2024

Automating End-to-End Insurance Claim Triage with Vision & Document Intelligence

AMAX Insurance (USA) · Solutions Architect
⬡ The Challenge & Serverless Solution

Legacy automobile insurance claim workflows were plagued by manual data entry, human error in police report transcription, and slow damage assessments that took 3–5 business days.

I architected a 100% serverless, event-driven ingestion and decision engine on AWS. When photos and police reports land in Amazon S3, an AWS Lambda orchestrator dispatches parallel workloads to Amazon Rekognition (for multi-angle vehicular damage classification) and Amazon Textract (for structured policy and police report OCR), feeding extracted signals into Amazon EventBridge for instant fast-track approval or adjuster escalation.

Professional Philosophy

Architecting for Reliability

Thirteen years of delivering systems for high-stakes environments has defined my architectural perspective. I prioritize visibility, recoverability, and long-term maintainability over transient industry trends.

Meaningful systems are defined by their ability to deliver consistent results in production. My architectural choices are validated by real-world performance, operational reliability, and client success.

// 02

Observability is Mandatory

Silent failures are unacceptable in complex systems. My design methodology mandates deep observability, ensuring that all failures are immediately detected, logged, and recoverable.

// 03

Resilience by Design

Robust architecture assumes infrastructure volatility. By designing for self-recovery and distributed reliability, my systems maintain continuity in challenging environments where typical architectures fail.

// 04

Architecture must be audit-ready by default. I prioritize clean, documented designs that simplify compliance reviews and provide long-term operational clarity for stakeholders and regulatory bodies.
